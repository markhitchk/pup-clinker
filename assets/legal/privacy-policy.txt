PUPPY CLICKER — PRIVACY POLICY
Effective: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Puppy Clicker, provided by Harley's Studios, handles information in the Android app.

1. LOCAL GAME DATA
Puppy Clicker stores game progress, settings, upgrade data, care statistics, achievements, PupEye fair-play state, and related gameplay information on the user's device.

2. PLAYER USERNAME
A player may choose a local Puppy Clicker username. The username is normalized to lowercase and is stored locally. It may also be included inside encrypted save files so the app can identify which local player a save belongs to.

3. DEVICE MODEL IN ENCRYPTED SAVES
Encrypted save metadata may contain a coarse manufacturer/model label for the Android device that created the save. This label is kept inside the encrypted save payload and is not intended to be displayed publicly. Puppy Clicker does not store IMEI, hardware serial number, Android ID, phone number, advertising ID, or other persistent hardware identifiers for save ownership.

4. SAVE ENCRYPTION
Automatic Android/data saves use authenticated encryption and Android Keystore protection. Portable backups may use password-based authenticated encryption. Authentication failures or unauthorized modifications may be recorded locally by PupEye and may cause a save to be rejected, quarantined, or restored from a last-known-good state.

5. PUPEYE FAIR-PLAY INFORMATION
PupEye may process recent tap timing, fair-play strike counts, save-integrity results, and related local security state to detect likely automated clicking or unauthorized save modification. These checks are designed to operate locally unless a future online feature expressly states otherwise.

6. STREAMED CONTENT AND NETWORK REQUESTS
Puppy Clicker may contact GitHub-hosted resources to retrieve streamed branding, legal documents, configuration, or other app assets. When the app makes those requests, the hosting provider may receive ordinary network information such as IP address, request time, and technical connection details under that provider's own policies.

7. DISCORD AND WEBSITE LOGIN
Discord and website login features are currently marked as coming soon. If account login or cloud syncing is introduced, this policy will be updated to explain any additional information collected, transmitted, or stored before those features are enabled for users.

8. DATA SHARING
Puppy Clicker does not sell local game-save data. Local usernames, encrypted save contents, and local PupEye state are not intentionally shared with advertisers. Third-party hosting providers may process normal network metadata when streamed resources are requested.

9. RETENTION AND DELETION
Local Puppy Clicker information remains on the device until removed by the user, cleared through Android app storage controls, overwritten by an authorized import, or removed when the app is uninstalled, subject to Android backup/storage behavior. Exported backup files remain wherever the user chooses to save them until the user deletes them.

10. CHILDREN AND AGE-SENSITIVE FEATURES
Puppy Clicker is designed as a casual game. Online account, social, or cloud features that may be added later will require an updated privacy review before launch.

11. SECURITY
Harley's Studios uses measures such as authenticated encryption, Android Keystore-backed keys for device-bound saves, password-protected portable backups, and PupEye integrity checks. No security system can guarantee protection against every form of device compromise or data loss.

12. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY
This policy may be updated when Puppy Clicker features, data practices, or connected services change. The current version displayed in the app is streamed from the official Puppy Clicker repository when available.

13. CONTACT
Questions about Puppy Clicker privacy may be directed to Harley's Studios through the official Puppy Clicker community or support channels made available in the app or official website.
